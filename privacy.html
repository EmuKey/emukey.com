Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

EmuKey LLC (“we”) is a limited liability company in the United States. This policy covers this website, emukey.com. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy that explains what the app does with your information:

What this website collects

Nothing about you directly. emukey.com has no accounts, forms, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or cookies, and we don’t receive any information about who visits it.

Services the website uses

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub receives your IP address and the usual details your browser sends, such as the page you asked for and your browser type, and may log them for security. GitHub handles them under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Everything else the site uses, including its typeface, is served from emukey.com itself, so no other company is contacted when you visit.

Stored in your browser

If you use the light or dark mode button, the site remembers your choice in your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it with your browser’s site data settings.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply and to support you. We don’t sell or share them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
