Products

DesktopWiki

Now in beta

A wiki for your family or small team, running on your own PC. Spaces, a page tree and a visual editor, the way classic team wikis work, with page history, attachments, search, accounts and permissions, and scheduled backups.

It’s one Windows program with no server, database or cloud account to set up. Anyone on your home or office network can read and edit pages from a browser or phone, and the whole wiki lives in a single file you can back up by copying it.

Visit desktopwiki.com or email support@desktopwiki.com to join the beta.

InCloseMail

Now in beta

A classic desktop email client for Microsoft 365. Folder pane, message list, and reading pane, laid out the way long-time desktop email users expect, running as a native Windows app.

Your mail stays between your computer and your Microsoft 365 tenant. InCloseMail signs in with your organization’s Microsoft account and doesn’t route your messages through our servers.

Visit inclosemail.com or email support@inclosemail.com to join the beta.
